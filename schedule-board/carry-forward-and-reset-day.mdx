description: Carry Forward leftover jobs, or Reset Day.
---

Both actions are icon buttons in the top bar next to **Auto Schedule**. Only Owner / Admin, General Manager, and Service Advisor see them, and only while billing is current.

  <img src="/images/schedule-board/day-actions.png" alt="The top bar buttons: Auto Schedule, the arrow-right Carry Forward icon, and the rotate Reset Day icon" />
</Frame>

## Carry Forward

Carry Forward sweeps every earlier day and moves uncompleted jobs onto the target day. The target is today if the shop is open, otherwise the next open day.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/schedule-board/carry-forward-dialog.png" alt="The Carry forward uncompleted jobs confirmation dialog with Cancel and Carry Forward buttons" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dialog">
    Click the arrow-right icon with the tooltip "Carry Forward Uncompleted Jobs", or press C on the board.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the confirmation">
    The dialog is titled "Carry forward uncompleted jobs?" and reads: "Move every uncompleted job scheduled before [target date] onto [target date]. BayBoard places each leftover on the same tech, packed from shop open into the earliest gap. It never overlaps a job or time-off already there. Anything that does not fit moves to Unassigned. Jobs already in Unassigned are not touched."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Carry Forward**. The board jumps to the target day if anything was placed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

What moves: jobs with status Assigned, In Progress, or Waiting on Parts on any earlier day.

What stays: Completed jobs, and jobs already in Unassigned.

Each leftover stays on the same technician. Carry Forward packs it from shop open into the earliest gap. That gap never overlaps a job or time-off already there.

If nothing fits, the job goes to Unassigned with an amber banner that starts with "Bumped".

The result toast reads "[n] scheduled · [m] bumped to Unassigned". One press handles up to 200 jobs. If more remain, the toast adds "more still to carry". Press C again.

Carry Forward has no Undo. To put a job back, drag it to its old slot or run Auto-Schedule.

## Reset Day

Reset Day takes every uncompleted job off the day you are viewing and returns it to Unassigned.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dialog">
    Click the rotate icon with the tooltip "Reset Day: move all jobs back to Unassigned", or press R on the board.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the confirmation">
    The dialog is titled "Reset the day?" and reads: "Every uncompleted job on [date] moves back to Unassigned. Completed jobs stay."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Reset Day**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

What moves: Assigned and In Progress jobs become In Queue. Waiting on Parts jobs keep their status. All of them go to Unassigned.

What stays: Completed and Archived jobs.

The result toast reads "[n] jobs moved to Unassigned". Reset Day has no Undo, so drag jobs back or run Auto-Schedule to rebuild the day.

If you are on a past day, you see "Past days are read-only, so this day cannot be reset." If the day is already empty, you see "Nothing to reset on [date]."

<Warning>
Check the date in the top bar before you confirm either action.
</Warning>
