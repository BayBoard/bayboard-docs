The Unassigned panel is the list on the right side of the board. It holds jobs that are not placed on a technician. The header reads "Unassigned Jobs" with the count and total hours.

## What lands here

- New jobs saved without both a Technician and a Tech Start Time.
- Jobs set to In Queue.
- Jobs set to Waiting on Parts that are not on the board.
- Jobs you unassign by dragging them off the board or clicking **Unassign** in Job Details.
- Jobs moved off the board by Reset Day.
- Jobs that Carry Forward or Auto-Schedule could not fit.

Archived jobs live here too, hidden behind a filter.

## Filters

Two filters sit under the header.

- **Job Type** shows all types or one type.
- **Status** shows all statuses or one. Choose Archived to see archived jobs so you can restore one.

## What a card shows

Each card shows the customer, vehicle, description, hours, a job-type chip, and a status pill. Two extras can appear:

- **"For [tech]" tag.** The job was saved with a technician but no start time. It is meant for that tech but not yet placed. Auto-Schedule prefers that tech for the job.
- **Warning triangle.** The promised time has passed and the job is not Completed. See [Promised time](/schedule-board/promised-time).

## Bumped banner

A job that was pushed off the board shows a banner in capitals across the top of the card. The text starts with "Bumped" and says why: Carry Forward could not re-place it, or a technician's new time off covered its slot (for example "Bumped, Bob R. off"). Drag it onto the board or run Auto-Schedule to place it. See [Carry Forward and Reset Day](/schedule-board/carry-forward-and-reset-day) and [Time off](/time-off).

## Collapse the panel

Click the chevron in the header to collapse the panel and widen the board. Click again to bring it back. Ctrl+B on Windows, Cmd+B on Mac, collapses the panel together with the navigation rail and Shop Performance.

## Add New Job

The **Add New Job** button sits at the bottom of the panel. It appears for Owner / Admin, General Manager, Service Advisor, and Foreman when billing is current. See [Add a job](/schedule-board/add-a-job).

## Past days

On a past day the header shows a "Past days are read-only." chip. You cannot drag from the panel, but you can still open a card, change its status, or archive it.
