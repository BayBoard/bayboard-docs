---
title: Time off
description: Block a technician out for a full day or part of a day.
---

A time-off block keeps work off a technician's column for a set window. Use it for vacation, sick days, appointments, or any time the technician is not on the floor.

## What a block does

- Jobs already on the board inside the block move to the Unassigned panel with a red banner.
- Auto-Schedule skips the blocked time.
- Drag and drop onto the blocked time is rejected with "That time is unavailable for this technician."
- The board shows a hatched overlay on the column with an OFF badge for a full day or PARTIAL for part of a day, plus the reason.

A block can be all day or part of a day, and can span up to 31 calendar days.

## Where to find it

The place to add time off depends on your role.

| Role | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Service Advisor, Foreman | Settings > **Time Off** tab |
| Owner / Admin, General Manager | Settings > **Team**, pick a technician, then the Time Off Management panel |

Technician cannot add or delete time off.
