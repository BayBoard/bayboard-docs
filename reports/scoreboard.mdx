Scoreboard is the second lens in Reports. Owner / Admin, General Manager, Service Advisor, and Foreman can open it.

</Frame>

## Pick a range

Use the "Report range" select. The options are Today, This pay period, This week, Last week, This month, Last month, Year to date, and Custom.

Custom adds "Custom range start" and "Custom range end" date inputs. A custom range can cover at most 366 days. A longer range shows "Custom ranges are limited to 366 days. Narrow the range to run this report."

This pay period follows the Pay Period Configuration in Settings > General. See [Shop profile and hours](/getting-started/shop-profile-and-hours).

## The seven tiles

The first row compares each number with the period before. For example, "Up 6.5 hours from the week before".

| Row | Tiles |
| --- | --- |
| Row 1, with prior-period comparison | Hours delivered, Cars finished, Capacity used, On-time promises |
| Row 2 | Hours per car, Time in shop, Parts wait |

Hover the info icon on a tile to see how it is calculated.

Each tile has a **View Jobs (n)** button that opens the jobs behind it.

## The four panels

- **Hours by day** is a bar chart. Click a bar to open the jobs from that day.
- **Tech scoreboard** lists each technician with name, tier, and a delivered-versus-goal hours bar. When the roster is empty it reads "No Techs on the roster yet."
- **What fills your bays** is a donut by job type. Click a segment to open the jobs of that type.
- **Returning customers** lists customers seen more than once in the range. When empty it reads "No customer came back twice in this period."

## Open the jobs behind a number

Clicking a bar, a segment, or **View Jobs (n)** opens a panel on the right. Click an RO # to open that job's Job Details. The panel shows the first 200 rows with **Show All [n]** to load the rest. A slice with no jobs reads "No jobs match this slice." Press Esc to close.

## Empty states

Before any work is finished, the lens reads "Once your team completes jobs, your Scoreboard fills in."
