---
title: Archive or restore
description: Take a technician off the board and restore later.
---

Archiving removes a technician's column from the board without deleting their record. Only Owner / Admin and General Manager can archive or restore. There is no delete. Archive is the only way to remove a technician.

## Archive a technician

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the technician in Settings > Team">
    Open Settings from the left rail, pick **Team**, and click the technician in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Archived">
    Under Roster status, check the **Archived** box.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/team-and-roles/archived-checkbox.png" alt="A technician's fields in Settings > Team with the Roster status Archived checkbox on the right" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    A dialog asks "Archive this technician?" with the text "This technician moves off the schedule board and out of Auto-Schedule. Their finished work stays in reports, and you can restore them here at any time." Click **Archive** to continue or **Cancel** to leave that technician on the roster.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Confirming only marks the change. Click **Save Changes** to apply it. The list entry then shows "Archived" next to that person's job title.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens

- The technician's column leaves the board.
- Auto-Schedule no longer places jobs on them.
- They no longer appear in the TECHNICIAN list for time off.
- Finished work stays in reports.

<Note>
A linked user account is not changed by archiving. To stop someone from signing in, [deactivate the account](/users-and-access/change-a-role-or-deactivate) in Settings > Users.
</Note>

## Restore a technician

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the archived technician">
    In Settings > Team, click the technician in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Uncheck Archived">
    Clear the **Archived** box. Restoring does not ask you to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Changes**. The column returns to the board.
  </Step>
</Steps>
