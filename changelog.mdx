## BayBoard v1.0

August 2026 · Launch

Everything that ships with the first release.

**Schedule board**

- Tech columns show open time, status, customer, and promise.
- Add jobs, drag them to a new time, and open job details without leaving the board.
- Change a job's status, time, technician, estimate, or promise.

**Bulk scheduling**

- Run Auto-Schedule to place ready unassigned jobs.
- Carry Forward moves older incomplete scheduled jobs to the next open shop day.

**Settings and team**

- Set up your shop profile, add technicians, and review How It Works in setup.
- Manage shop hours, technicians, job types, statuses, and team access in Settings.
- Set technician skills and the preferences Auto-Schedule reads.
- Block a technician for a full day or part of a day. Jobs inside a new block move to Unassigned, and Auto-Schedule skips the blocked time on that tech.

**Reports**

- Open Today, Scoreboard, or My Hours for shop numbers.
- Choose a date range, open job details, and use CSV or print export by role.

**Support**

- Ask BayBoard, Contact Support, System Status, Shortcuts, and Legal are in Support.
- Documentation and What's New are buttons on Ask BayBoard. They open this help site in a new tab.
- Ask BayBoard answers from the published help docs. It does not read shop data.
- Open the shortcut list any time to see every key in BayBoard.

Later updates appear above this entry, newest first, with their version number.
