## BayBoard v1.0

August 2026 · Launch

Everything that ships with the first release.

**Schedule board**

- See each technician's day, open time, job status, customer, and promised time in one schedule.
- Add jobs, drag them to a new time, and open job details without leaving the board.
- Change a job's status, scheduled time, technician, estimate, or promised time from Job Details.

**Bulk scheduling**

- Run Auto-Schedule to place ready unassigned jobs into open technician time.
- Carry Forward moves older incomplete scheduled jobs to the next open shop day.

**Settings and team**

- Set up your shop profile, add technicians, and review How It Works in a guided three-step setup.
- Manage shop hours, technicians, job types, statuses, and team access in Settings.
- Set technician skills and scheduling preferences that guide Auto-Schedule.
- Block a technician for a full day or part of a day. Jobs inside a new block move to Unassigned, and Auto-Schedule skips the blocked time.

**Reports**

- Open Today, Scoreboard, or My Hours to see the numbers for your shop.
- Choose a date range, open job details, and use CSV or print export when your role allows it.

**Support**

- Use Support for guides, keyboard shortcuts, release notes, and a contact form.
- Open the shortcut list any time to see every key BayBoard supports.
