title: Invite a user
description: Send a sign-in invite with a role and roster link.
---

Accounts are created by invitation. Owner / Admin and General Manager send invites from Settings > Users. Foreman can open the Users tab but sees "View only. User access is visible from your role but cannot be changed."

</Frame>

## Send the invite

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings > Users">
    Open Settings from the left rail and pick **Users**. The header shows "Users (n)".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite User">
    Click **Invite User**. The dialog opens with the note "The invite expires in seven days."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the fields">
    - **FULL NAME** The person's name, 100 characters or fewer.
    - **EMAIL (FOR LOGIN)** The address they will sign in with. The invite is sent to this email address on file.
    - **ROLE** General Manager, Service Advisor, Foreman, or Technician. Pick one role.

    An Owner / Admin cannot be invited. Each shop has one owner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link a roster tech (Foreman and Technician only)">
    When you choose Foreman or Technician, a LINK TO ROSTER TECH field appears. It lists "Not linked" and every roster technician that is not already linked to an account.

    The app explains: "This link, not the email address, connects the account to a schedule column. Pick the roster record this person works. Selecting none is allowed, but the person will see setup-incomplete until linked."

    If they are not on the roster yet, add them in Settings > Team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Send Invite">
    Click **Send Invite**. On success you see "The invitation email to [email] is on its way." The person now appears in the Users list with an Invite pending chip.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Validation messages you may see: "Enter a full name with 100 characters or fewer.", "Enter a valid email address.", and "Choose a role."

## Resend or revoke an invite

Click a pending invite in the Users list. The detail shows Full name, Email, Role, Sent, Expires, and an Intended roster tech card.

- **Resend Invite** emails a fresh link. The confirm reads "Resend [name]'s invite?" Click confirm to send it.
- **Revoke Invite** cancels the invite. The confirm reads "Revoke [name]'s invite?" Click confirm to cancel it.

The app notes: "Resending emails a new link and expires the previous one, so the invitee always has exactly one that works. Revoking cancels the emailed link outright."

<Note>
Invites expire after seven days. If one expires, send a new invite to the same email.
</Note>
