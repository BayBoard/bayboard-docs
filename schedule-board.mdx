The schedule board is the home screen. It shows one day of work for every technician in the shop. Use it to add jobs, place them on technicians, and track status through the day.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/schedule-board/board-overview.png" alt="Schedule board grid with technician columns, the time column, capacity chips, and job cards" />
</Frame>

## What the board shows

- **Technician columns.** Each active technician gets a column. The header shows the short name, a tier label (Foreman, A Tech, B Tech, Apprentice), and a "You" pill on the column that is yours.
- **Time rows.** Time runs down the left side in 30-minute ticks, from when the shop opens to when it closes.
- **Capacity chip.** Each column header shows open hours for the day, such as "3.5h open" or "1.0h over". Red means under 30 minutes open, amber under 2 hours, and green when more than two hours remain.
- **Job cards.** Each card shows the customer, vehicle, RO number, hours, job type, and a status pill. Click a card to open Job Details.
- **Time off.** A hatched overlay with an "OFF" or "PARTIAL" badge marks time a technician is away.
- **Unassigned panel.** The list on the right side of the board. Jobs wait here until they are placed on a technician.
- **Shop Performance strip.** The strip at the bottom shows hours completed against the shop goal, assigned hours, and Goal, Assigned, and Done for each technician.

## Moving between days

Use the chevrons in the top bar to go to the previous or next open day. Closed days are skipped. Click the date to pick a day from the calendar. **Today** jumps back to the current day.

Past days and future days are read-only for placement. You cannot drag jobs on them. Status changes, edits, archive, and unassign still work on past boards.

<Tip>
Press Ctrl+B on Windows, Cmd+B on Mac, to collapse the navigation rail, Shop Performance, and the Unassigned panel together. Press it again to bring them back.
</Tip>

## Who can do what

- **Every role** can view the board, search, and open Job Details.
- **Add, edit, drag, resize, change status, archive, or unassign a job.** Owner / Admin, General Manager, Service Advisor, or Foreman can do this action.
- **Auto Schedule, Carry Forward, and Reset Day.** Owner / Admin, General Manager, or Service Advisor can do this action.

Technicians see the board as read-only. If billing is past due, the board is read-only for everyone.
