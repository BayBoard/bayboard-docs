BayBoard runs on a few roles. Each one has a short list of things to do on the first day.

## Day one by role

- **Owner / Admin or General Manager.** Set up the shop profile and hours, add technicians, and invite users.
- **Service Advisor or Foreman.** Add and assign jobs on the board.
- **Technician.** View the board and check My hours in Reports.

The Owner / Admin completes the three-step setup first. After that, Service Advisors and Foremen can start putting jobs on the board as soon as they accept their invite.
