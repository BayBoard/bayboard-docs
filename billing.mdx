Billing is an Owner / Admin screen. Open Settings and pick **Billing** in the left list. Other roles do not see the tab.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/billing/settings-billing.png" alt="Settings > Billing showing the Current plan card and the Open Billing Portal button" />
</Frame>

## Current plan

The "Current plan" card shows:

- The plan name, Founders or Standard.
- A status pill: Trial, Active, Past due, Canceled, or Expired.
- The price, one of $99/mo, $999/year, $199/mo, or $1,990/year.
- "Trial ends [date]" while you are on a trial.
- "Next billing: [date]".

## Update your card, bank account, or billing details

The "Payment method" card reads: "Update your card, Link account, bank account, invoices, and billing details in the Stripe Customer Portal."

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Open Billing Portal">
    The Stripe Customer Portal opens in a new tab. The button notes "Opens Stripe Customer Portal in a new tab."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes in the portal">
    Update the card or bank account on file, download invoices, or edit billing details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to BayBoard">
    Close the portal tab when you are done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the portal cannot open, you see "Billing portal could not be opened." Try again, and if it still fails, [contact support](/support/contact-support). A non-owner who reaches this screen sees "Only the shop owner can manage billing."

## Trial banner

During a trial, a banner at the top of the app reads "[n] days left in your trial." Its **Manage Billing** link opens Settings > Billing.

## Past due

If a payment fails, a banner reads "Payment past due. Update your payment method to restore full access." with an **Update Payment** link.

<Warning>
While billing is past due the whole app is read-only for every role, including the Owner / Admin. Staff can still see the board, but cannot add or edit jobs, drag cards, run Auto-Schedule, or change any setting. The Unassigned panel shows "Payment past due. Update your payment method to restore full access." Update the payment method in the Stripe Customer Portal to restore full access.
</Warning>
