Settings > Notifications controls your own in-app alerts. Every role has this section. Each toggle saves as soon as you change it. There is no Save button.

Alerts show under the bell icon in the top bar.

## In-app alerts

Defaults depend on your role. Owner starts with Promised time approaching off, and with End-of-day wrap off.

The End-of-day wrap title in Settings includes the scope for your role: **Your day** for Technician, **The floor** for Foreman, **The shop** for Owner / Admin, General Manager, and Service Advisor.

| Alert | When it fires | Who sees it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Promised time at risk | Time remaining before the promised time is under the remaining hours plus 1 hour. Fixed rule, no guesswork. | All roles |
| Promised time approaching | 60 minutes remain before a promised time. Owner default is off. | All roles |
| Waiting customer not started | A waiting job has been on the board 15 minutes without a start. | All roles except Technician |
| Jobs carried forward | Jobs were moved to a later day by Carry Forward. | All roles |
| End-of-day wrap | A summary after closing time. Hours completed vs. goal, jobs completed, and jobs carried over. | All roles. Owner default is off. |
| Same-day time-off bump | A job was moved to Unassigned by time off added for today. | All roles |
| Job assigned to you | Someone else put a job on your column. This toggle also covers Auto-Schedule. | Foreman and Technician. Not shown for other roles. |

Auto-Schedule uses a separate bell titled "Jobs scheduled for you". It is one summary for that run, such as "3 scheduled". It uses the same **Job assigned to you** Settings toggle. Assigning a job to yourself does not fire this alert.

## Send a test

Click **Send A Test** to create one notification for yourself. It does not notify anyone else. You see "Test notification sent." and the alert appears under the bell.
