---
title: My profile and password
description: Update your name or change your password in Settings > My Profile.
---

Settings > My Profile is where you manage your own account. Every role has this section. You can also reach it from the account menu at the bottom of the left rail by clicking **My Profile**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/getting-started/settings-my-profile.png" alt="Settings > My Profile showing the Profile panel and the Change Password panel" />
</Frame>

## Update your name

<Steps>
  <Step title="Edit Full Name">
    In the Profile panel, change Full Name. This is the name other staff see.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Profile**. You see "Changes saved."
  </Step>
</Steps>

Email is read-only. It is managed by your shop admin in Users. Ask your Owner / Admin or General Manager if it needs to change.

## Change your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your current password">
    In the Change Password panel, type your Current Password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a new password">
    Type a New Password of at least 8 characters, then type it again in Confirm New Password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update">
    Click **Update Password**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Error | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| "Use at least 8 characters." | Pick a longer New Password. |
| "The passwords do not match." | Retype Confirm New Password so it matches. |
| "The current password is incorrect." | Retype Current Password. If you cannot remember it, sign out and use **Forgot password?**. |
