BayBoard accounts come from an invite. There is no self-serve signup. If you do not have an account yet, ask your Owner / Admin or General Manager to invite you, then follow [Accept an invite](/users-and-access/accept-an-invite).

## Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign-in page">
    Go to the BayBoard sign-in page. It reads "Welcome back." and "Sign in to your shop."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email and password">
    Type the Email you were invited with and your Password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose whether to stay signed in">
    **Keep me signed in** starts checked. Leave it checked on your own device. Uncheck it on a shared shop computer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Sign In">
    Click **Sign In**. You land on the schedule board.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/getting-started/sign-in.png" alt="The BayBoard sign-in form with Email, Password, Keep me signed in, Forgot password?, and the Sign In button" />
</Frame>

If you see "That email and password did not match an active account.", check the spelling of both. If it still fails, reset your password below, or ask your shop admin whether your account is active.

## Forgot your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Forgot password?">
    On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?**. The page reads "Reset your password."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the reset link">
    Enter your Email and click **Send Reset Link**. The page changes to "Check your email." It reads "If an account uses [email], a password reset link is on its way."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the link and choose a new password">
    Open the email and click the link. On the "Choose a new password." page, enter and confirm your new password. When you see "Password updated.", sign in with the new password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/getting-started/forgot-password.png" alt="The Reset your password page with the Email field, Send Reset Link button, and Back To Sign In link" />
</Frame>

If the page says "This reset link expired.", it also says "Reset links are time limited for security. Request a new one to continue." Click **Send A New Reset Link**.

## Messages you might see

- **"Your account has been deactivated."** Your shop admin turned off your access. Ask your Owner / Admin or General Manager to turn it back on.
- **"You've been signed out"** Your session ended. Click **Sign Back In** and sign in with your email again.
- **"You don't have access"** That page is not available for your role. Click **Back to Schedule**. Contact your shop admin if you think this is a mistake.
