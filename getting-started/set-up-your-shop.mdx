The first time an Owner / Admin or General Manager signs in to a new shop, BayBoard opens a guided setup. The header reads "Setup · Step 1 of 3". Finish it before inviting the team.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Shop Profile">
    Fill in the shop details. Shop Name, Street Address, City, State, ZIP, and Phone are required. DBA / Legal Name is optional.

    Under Hours of operation, set when each day opens and closes. These hours become the schedule grid on the board. Set Monday first, then click **Use Monday's Hours Tue-Fri** to copy it to the rest of the work week.

    Under Timezone and pay period, pick your Timezone. Jobs land on the calendar day for this timezone. Then choose the pay period Frequency and Start Day. The pay period feeds the My hours report.

    Click **Continue**. The button reads "Fill required fields to continue" until every required field is filled.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Team">
    Add the people on your board. Each person becomes a column on the schedule.

    In Add someone to the board, enter Full Name, Short Name, Tier (Foreman, A Tech, B Tech, or Apprentice), Daily Hours, and a Color. Click **Add Technician**. Repeat for each person. The Team list shows everyone you have added.

    This is the roster only. Account access is managed separately, after setup, in Settings > Users. One Tech or Foreman is enough to start. You cannot continue until at least one technician is added.
  </Step>
  <Step title="How it Works">
    Review how BayBoard organizes work. Type is what the work is. Status is where it is now. Both lists are fixed and ready to use. Open the Job Types and Statuses accordions to see them.

    Check **I agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy**, then click **Finish Setup**. Use **Back** if you need to change an earlier step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After Finish Setup, the owner chooses a plan and starts the trial. You then see "You're in, [FirstName]. [Shop] is live on BayBoard." Click **Open The Board**.

## Your first visit to the board

A short Board Tour appears the first time you open the board. It walks through the main parts of the screen, such as the technician columns. Use **Next** and **Back** to step through it, or **Skip Tour** to close it.

<Tip>
Press ? on the board to see keyboard shortcuts, or press Ctrl+/ on Windows, Cmd+/ on Mac, to open Support at any time.
</Tip>
