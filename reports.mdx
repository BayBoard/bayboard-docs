---
title: Reports
description: See the shop's numbers in three lenses, Today, Scoreboard, and My hours.
---

Reports lives in the left rail. Press G then R to jump there from anywhere. The page has three lenses, shown as tabs at the top.

- **Today** is a live view of the board right now: what is on it, what is at risk, and what is stuck.
- **Scoreboard** shows delivered hours, cars finished, and capacity over a date range, compared with the prior period.
- **My hours** shows one technician's own hours, pay period, and on-time rate.

## Who sees which lens

| Role | Today | Scoreboard | My hours |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Owner / Admin | Yes | Yes | No |
| General Manager | Yes | Yes | No |
| Service Advisor | Yes | Yes | No |
| Foreman | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Technician | No | No | Yes |

Opening Reports lands on Today. Technicians land on My hours.

## Pick a range

Today has no range picker. It shows "Live · as of [time]" with a **Refresh** button, and it refreshes again when you return to the tab.

Scoreboard and My hours have a "Report range" select with these options: Today, This pay period, This week, Last week, This month, Last month, Year to date, Custom. Custom adds "Custom range start" and "Custom range end" date inputs. A custom range can cover at most 366 days.

## Open the jobs behind a number

Every tile has a **View Jobs (n)** button. Bars and donut segments are clickable too. Each one opens a drill panel on the right that lists the jobs in that slice. Click an RO # in the list to open that job's Job Details. Press Esc to close the panel.

## Export and print

Owner / Admin sees **Export CSV** and **Print** in the Reports header on Today and Scoreboard. See [Export and print](/reports/export-and-print).

## If the report cannot load

You see "Reports could not load. Check your connection and reload." with a **Reload** button.
