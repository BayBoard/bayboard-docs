---
title: Team and roles
description: How the roster, job titles, and accounts fit.
---

BayBoard keeps two separate lists of people. The technician roster decides who has a column on the schedule board. User accounts decide who can sign in. A person can be on one list, the other, or both.

## Roster technician vs user account

A roster technician is a record in Settings > Team. Each one creates a column on the board, and Auto-Schedule places work into that column. A roster record does not sign in. It has no email or password.

A user account is a record in Settings > Users. It gives a person sign-in access with a role. When you invite a Foreman or Technician, you can link the account to a roster technician. That link, not the email address, connects the account to a schedule column.

- **Add a column to the board.** Go to Settings > Team.
- **Give someone a sign-in.** Go to Settings > Users.
- **Link a sign-in to a column.** Go to Settings > Users, then use LINK TO ROSTER TECH when you send the invite.

## Job titles

Every roster technician has one of four job titles. The board shows a short form of it under the technician's short name.

| Job title | Board label |
| --- | --- |
| Foreman | Foreman |
| A Technician | A Tech |
| B Technician | B Tech |
| Apprentice | Apprentice |

The job title does not change what a person can do in the app. Access comes from the role on their user account.

## Column order

Columns on the board follow the order of the roster in Settings > Team. Drag a technician in the list, or use **Move Up** and **Move Down**, to change the order.

## Who can edit the roster

Owner / Admin and General Manager can add, edit, reorder, and archive technicians. Service Advisor and Foreman can open Settings > Team but see it view only. Technician does not see the Team tab.
